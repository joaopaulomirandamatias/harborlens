# SPEC-007: Commercial managed service (conditional)

**Status:** PROPOSED; NOT BUILT · **Created:** 2026-11-08.

## Problem / user story

Potential organizational users may value managed analyses, private operational inputs and secure reporting.

## Linked requirements

FR-019..020; NFR-008, NFR-017..019; BR-015..018

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** real buyers confirm recurring problems and budget; **when** commercial scope is selected; **then** a narrowly scoped pilot proposal ties delivery to measured value.
2. **Given** two customer organizations are configured; **when** data are accessed; **then** authorization tests prevent cross-tenant access.
3. **Given** private or licensed AIS data is connected; **when** ingestion begins; **then** contract/retention/export rights are confirmed first.

## Non-goals and safety

No operational navigation, autonomous control, live vessel tracking, regulatory certification, unverified berth or waiting-time claims, or unauthorized licensed data redistribution. A synthetic fixture is not independent validation.

## Definition of Done

Accepted and reviewed requirements, passing boundary/negative tests, documented source rights, updated JSON schema and traceability, explicit uncertainty and reproducible evidence attached to the linked issue/PR.
